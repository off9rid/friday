Your notes stay on your Mac.

Friday lives on your own computer and only listens when you hold the key. Your vault isn't stored on our servers. Only your question, and the few notes needed to answer it, go to the AI model. Read exactly how it works.

Questions.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We do the setup with you on a call. If you can install an app, you can use Friday.

What if I don't use Obsidian?

We'll create a vault for you during setup and bring in what you already have: notes, docs and your calendar.

Does it work on Windows or my phone?

Friday runs on Mac today. Windows and phone versions are on the roadmap.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Siri?

They don't know your life. Friday is built on your own notes, deadlines and routines, and it takes actions on your Mac instead of only answering.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. The setup fee covers building yours; the monthly plan stops whenever you want.
