Your notes stay on your Mac.

Friday lives on your own computer. Your vault isn't uploaded or stored on our servers. Only the question you're asking is sent to the AI model to get an answer.

Questions.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We do the setup with you on a call. If you can install an app, you can use Friday.

What if I don't use Obsidian?

We'll create a vault for you during setup and bring in what you have: notes, docs, your calendar.

Does it work on Windows or my phone?

Friday runs on Mac today. Windows and phone versions are on the roadmap.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Siri?

They don't know your life. Friday is built on your own notes, deadlines and routines, and it takes actions on your Mac instead of just answering.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. The setup fee covers our time building yours; the monthly plan stops whenever you want.
