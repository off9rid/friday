Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

The short version

  • Friday is not always listening. She only listens when you hold the Option key, click her, or use her in the notch. When the microphone is on, macOS shows its orange microphone indicator.
  • Your notes stay on your Mac. We don't copy your notes to our servers.
  • But answers need an AI model. When you ask something, your question and the few notes needed to answer it are sent to an AI provider, then discarded by us.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't run ads.
  • On this website we only collect what you type into the sign-up form.

1. Who we are

Friday is a personal AI assistant for Mac, operated by [Business name], based in Ontario, Canada. Questions about privacy go to [contact email].

2. When Friday listens

Friday does not record in the background. The microphone is only active while you are holding the activation key, or after you deliberately start a conversation by hovering over or clicking Friday in the notch. When you let go, the microphone stops. We do not keep audio recordings. Your speech is turned into text so Friday can understand the request, and that text is what is used from then on.

Friday can only act on your Mac in ways you allow. macOS asks for your permission before Friday can use the microphone, read your screen, control other apps or access your files, and you can turn any of those off in System Settings at any time.

3. What we collect on this website

  • The information you type into the sign-up form: your name, email address and the plan you are interested in.
  • A record that you agreed to these Terms and this Privacy Policy, with the date and time.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

4. What happens with your data when you use Friday

  • Your notes and files stay on your computer. Friday reads them locally.
  • Your questions are sent, together with the small pieces of your notes needed to answer them, to an AI model provider so it can write a reply. We choose providers that do not use your content to train their models where that option exists, but each provider has its own policy, which we will name for you during setup.
  • Spoken replies are generated for you from the text of the answer.
  • Setup call and questionnaire: the answers you give us about your routine and goals are used only to set up your Friday and are kept as long as you are a customer.

Because answers depend on an AI model, please don't ask Friday to handle information you aren't comfortable sending to an AI provider, such as passwords, health records or other people's confidential data.

5. Who else sees your information

We use a small number of service providers to run Friday: an AI model provider, a form and email service for the sign-up list, and a hosting provider for this website. They may only use your information to provide their service to us. We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may disclose it if the law requires us to.

6. How long we keep it

Sign-up details are kept until you ask us to delete them or you stop being a customer, and for up to 12 months after that for record keeping. Server logs are kept for a short period set by the hosting provider.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, withdraw your consent, or have it deleted. Email [contact email] and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in Canada you can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Young people

If you are under 18, please get a parent or guardian's permission before signing up or using Friday.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, such as limiting who can access it and using encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you promptly if a breach affects you.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and let you know by email before it takes effect.
